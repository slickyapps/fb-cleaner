● FB CLEANER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

FB Cleaner is a tool for managing comments on Facebook Pages that a user is authorized to administer. This policy explains the information the tool may access and how it is used.

1. Information accessed

When you connect a Facebook account and authorize the requested permissions, FB Cleaner may access your account's basic identification information, the list of Pages you manage, Page posts, comments and related metadata, and access tokens required to make authorized Meta API requests. The exact information available depends on the permissions you grant and Meta's API restrictions.

2. How information is used

Information is used to display connected Pages and comments, help authorized administrators review comments, and carry out moderation actions that administrators explicitly select. FB Cleaner does not require access to your Facebook password.

3. Storage and security

FB Cleaner may store connected-account information, API tokens, Page identifiers, moderation settings and action logs in its configured application environment. The operator is responsible for restricting access to the application and its storage, keeping credentials confidential, and maintaining appropriate backups and security controls. Data should not be stored longer than necessary for the tool's operation.

4. Sharing

FB Cleaner communicates with Meta's APIs to provide the requested functionality. The tool is not intended to sell personal information or use Facebook Page data for advertising profiles. If the deployment uses third-party infrastructure or services, the operator should disclose those services and any applicable data transfers before use.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can revoke the application's permissions through your Facebook settings. To request deletion of stored account data, tokens, Page associations or logs, contact the application operator using the contact method provided to you when the service is deployed. Revoking Meta access may not automatically erase locally stored data.

6. Retention

Information is retained only as needed for legitimate moderation operations, security, troubleshooting, and applicable legal obligations. Specific retention periods depend on the deployment and should be documented by its operator.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact the FB Cleaner operator through the contact information supplied with your access to the service. Before submitting this policy to Meta, the operator must add a functioning public contact email or request form here.

FB Cleaner is an independent tool and is not endorsed by Meta.
